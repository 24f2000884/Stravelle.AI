WHAT WE BELIEVE

A manifesto for an AI-literate world.

01

A right, not a privilege.

AI literacy shouldn't be reserved for technologists. We're building tools and understanding for individuals, families, workplaces, and institutions.

02

Technology-driven, human-centered.

Our mission is to help society understand, adopt, and use AI with confidence. We pair rigorous technology with a deeply human approach to learning and change.

03

Evidence before initiatives.

We're working to understand the real-world state of AI awareness, readiness, and adoption across communities and industries — engaging people, organizations, and stakeholders to build insights that shape what we do.

04

Beyond conventional approaches.

AI literacy cannot be built through conventional methods alone. We connect AI to everyday decisions, professional workflows, and real-world experiences to make it understandable, relevant, and actionable.
