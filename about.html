ABOUT STRAVELLE

We believe understanding comes before adoption.

Stravelle Private Limited is a technology-driven, human-centered company working to build an AI-literate society — one where individuals, families, businesses, educators, and policymakers can understand, question, and use AI responsibly.

OUR STORY

Started with a simple observation.

AI was moving faster than most people's understanding of it. Households were adopting tools without guidance, businesses were deploying systems without readiness, and classrooms were encountering AI without a framework for it. Stravelle was founded in New Delhi to close that gap — through research, education, and practical, human-first initiatives.

01

Clarity over hype

We explain what AI actually does — no jargon, no exaggerated claims, just practical understanding.

02

People before systems

Every initiative starts with the humans affected, not the technology being deployed.

03

Research-led

We study real-world awareness and readiness before designing any programme or workshop.

OUR MISSION

An AI-literate society, by 2030.

We're working toward a future where understanding AI is as fundamental as digital literacy is today — where families make informed choices, businesses adopt responsibly, schools teach practically, and institutions govern thoughtfully.
