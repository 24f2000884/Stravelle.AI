OUR STORY

Why AI literacy, and why now.

Closing the gap

Millions of people in India are left out of the systems meant to serve them. We build grassroots, technology-enabled solutions to close that gap.

Literacy for everyone

AI literacy should be foundational — not just for technical professionals, but for individuals, families, workplaces, and institutions navigating a changing world.

Confidence through understanding

We help society understand, adopt, and use AI with confidence, pairing rigorous technology with a genuinely human-centered approach.
